# Experiment map

The canonical map of this dissertation's experiments is maintained in
[`experiments/README.md`](experiments/README.md).

Use that index when referring to an experiment in writing, figures, notebooks,
or discussion. The existing `results/`, `configs/`, and `scripts/` paths are
retained as implementation paths. Historical checkpoint and log paths remain
recorded in configs, while their heavyweight payloads are excluded from GitHub.

The machine-readable mapping is in
[`experiments/registry.yaml`](experiments/registry.yaml).

Completed compact artifacts are grouped in [`artifacts/`](artifacts/). Legacy
paths under `results/` remain valid compatibility links.
