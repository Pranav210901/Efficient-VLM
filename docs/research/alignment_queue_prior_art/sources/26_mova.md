# MoVA

- Paper: *MoVA: Adapting Mixture of Vision Experts to Multimodal Context*
- Year/status: 2024 arXiv preprint
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13046
- Verified evidence: context-aware routing and fusion of specialised pretrained
  vision experts in an MLLM.
- Short excerpt: “adaptively routing and fusing task-specific vision experts.”
- Relevance: routing is not new, although its generative-MLLM setting differs.
