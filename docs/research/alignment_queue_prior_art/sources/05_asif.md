# ASIF

- Paper: *ASIF: Coupled Data Turns Unimodal Models to Multimodal Without
  Training*
- Venue: NeurIPS 2023
- URL: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/3186591903d9db31770ad131adb5ceb4-Abstract-Conference.html
- Verified evidence: connects unimodal models through paired anchors without
  ordinary multimodal training.
- Short excerpt: “transform unimodal models into a single multimodal model.”
- Relevance: training-free alternative and evidence of latent cross-modal
  compatibility.
