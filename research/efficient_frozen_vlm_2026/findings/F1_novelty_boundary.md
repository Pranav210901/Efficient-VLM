# F1 — Novelty boundary

Plain resolution reduction, token pruning/merging, early exit, cascades,
quantization, Matryoshka dimensions and distillation are established. The
defensible research object is narrower:

> Query-independent, cross-modal-neighbourhood-preserving conditional token
> computation inside a frozen SSL vision encoder, aligned to a separately
> pretrained frozen sentence encoder with under 5M trainable parameters.

Confidence: medium, not a novelty guarantee. The exact claim requires another
targeted search immediately before paper submission because July 2026 work is
still appearing.
