# S12 — SaMer

- Source: July 2026 preprint
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04605
- Credibility: medium pending peer review

SaMer is a close recent collision: frozen vision and language encoders,
projection-only adaptation, token compression and retrieval. Its multi-vector
late-interaction and post-encoder storage/scoring setting differs from this
project’s single global embedding and goal of reducing encoder execution, but
it prevents broad claims about being the first frozen-encoder retrieval
compression method.
