# S01 — Frozen unimodal alignment

- Source: CVPR 2025
- URL: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Maniparambil_Harnessing_Frozen_Unimodal_Encoders_for_Flexible_Multimodal_Alignment_CVPR_2025_paper.html
- Credibility: high

The paper directly establishes projector-based alignment between frozen,
independently pretrained encoders. Its reported best configuration uses DINOv2
and All-RoBERTa-Large. This is the closest foundation to the dissertation and
means “frozen experts plus projector” cannot itself be the novelty.
